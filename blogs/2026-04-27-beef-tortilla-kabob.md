In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, shredded cheese, bread crumbs, garlic, red bell pepper, parsley, bbq rub and Bachan’s Original Japanese Barbecue Sauce. Mix all of the ingredients thoroughly so that everything is fully combined. Stack all of the tortillas together, then cut them into a large square removing the rounded edges of the tortilla.
