Marinate shrimps with Bachan’s chili sesame sauce, red chili flake, sesame seeds, let marinate for about 30minutes Combine ginger, brown sugar, apple cider vinegar, and lime juice and mix until sugar has dissolved. Slice 1/2 of a sour green apple and mix with cabbage slaw and dressing, add in green onions and mix well Grill or sauté shrimps for about 3-3.5 minutes
