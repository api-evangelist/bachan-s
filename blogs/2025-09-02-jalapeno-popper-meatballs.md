Finely slice up the bacon then cook it in a skillet until crispy. In a bowl, combine burger,crispy bacon, onion, diced jalapeño, shredded cheese, bread crumbs, eggs and SPG blend.Hand mix all of the ingredients thoroughly. Take an ice cream scoop full of the mixture, place it on wax paper then gently flatten it out with your hand.
